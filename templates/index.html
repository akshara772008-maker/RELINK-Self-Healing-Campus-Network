HOW IT WORKS

From failure to recovery

01

Detect

The system continuously checks devices and links for failures.

02

Analyze

The routing engine removes failed components from the active network.

03

Reroute

Dijkstra or Bellman-Ford calculates an alternate path.

04

Recover

Packets continue through the recovered route.
